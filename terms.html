The short version

BOM is for saying kind things to friends. Be kind, don't misuse it, and we'll look after it. By using BOM you agree to these terms and to our privacy policy. Apple's standard licence terms for App Store apps also apply.

Who runs BOM

BOM is run by Ho Chung Au (United Kingdom). Contact: bomapphelp@gmail.com.

Who can use it

You must be 16 or over in the United Kingdom and Hong Kong, and 18 or over everywhere else (judged by your App Store country on iPhone, and by your SIM card's country or your region setting on Android). If you are 16 or 17, BOMs from adults always show their name.

Zero tolerance

BOM has ready-made messages only. Your display name must not be offensive, hateful, sexual, threatening or pretend to be someone else, and must not include contact details (web addresses, social media handles, emails or phone numbers) or anyone else's personal details. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users: we may remove names and content, and suspend or delete accounts, that break these terms. Report anything wrong from the message's ⋯ menu; we act on reports promptly.

Safety and complaints

To report a BOM, open it, tap ⋯ and choose a reason. We check reports regularly and remove illegal content within 48 hours of becoming aware of it. BOM is not for anyone under its minimum age (16 in the United Kingdom and Hong Kong, 18 elsewhere): if someone seems to be under it, report one of their BOMs and choose that reason, and we may suspend their account. For any other complaint, or to appeal if we removed your name or suspended your account, email bomapphelp@gmail.com. We reply within 2 days.

Don't misuse BOM

Don't harass anyone, send BOMs in bulk, try to find out who sent an anonymous BOM, fake activity to collect wings or badges, use bots or automation, or try to break, copy or reverse-engineer the app or its server.

Anonymity has limits

BOMs you share yourself show who you are. Anonymous BOMs don't show your identity to the recipient, but we will disclose it if the law requires us to, for example to the police.

Angel wings and badges have no cash value and can't be sold or transferred. If we offer rewards in future, they will come with their own terms.
