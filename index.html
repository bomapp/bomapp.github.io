Kindness that comes back

BOM friends

When someone thanks you for a BOM you shared, they can choose to become BOM friends. You can also send an invite link through WhatsApp, Instagram or any app (each link adds one person, within 24 hours), or let them scan your QR code.

Then you can send each other praise anonymously: it only says it's from a friend.

Angel wings

If a BOM helped, the person who got it can thank you with an angel wing. A quiet way to say thank you.

Praise collection

Every kind of praise you receive is collected in one place, private to you: something to come back to on a bad day.
