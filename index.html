Send a friend a kind word

BOM is an app for iPhone and Android for sending friends short, ready-made notes of praise — with your name, or anonymously to friends already on BOM.

BOM 是一個 iPhone 及 Android 應用程式，讓你用預先寫好的短句讚賞朋友——可以署名，亦可以匿名傳送給已使用 BOM 的朋友。
