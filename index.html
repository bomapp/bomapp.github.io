Send a friend a kind word

BOM is an app for iPhone and Android for sending friends short, ready-made notes of praise — with your name, or anonymously to friends already on BOM.

Coming soon to Hong Kong, the UK, the US and Taiwan.

How it works

1. Pick a kind word

24 ready-made notes of praise, each in a casual and a work tone.

2. Send it

Share it through WhatsApp, Messages or any app with your name, or send it anonymously to a BOM friend.

3. Get thanked

If it helped, they thank you with an angel wing.

BOM friends

When someone thanks you for a BOM you shared, they can choose to become BOM friends; scanning your QR code works too. Then you can send each other praise anonymously: it only says it's from a friend. Anyone can block a sender quietly, without finding out who it was.

Praise collection

Every kind of praise you receive is collected in one place, private to you: something to come back to on a bad day.

Weather friends and animal friends

Pick a weather friend or an animal friend, each inspired by one of the 16 personality types. Every one brings its own good energy, and each BOM you send makes it grow: its home gets more beautiful, and it grows markings, a glow and a charm from the way you praise people, so no two are the same. It only ever grows: no streaks, and nothing is lost in a quiet week.
