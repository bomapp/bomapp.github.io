We reply within a few days. Please include your anonymous ID (Settings → Profile in the app) if your question is about your data.
我們會在數日內回覆。如查詢與你的資料有關，請附上你的匿名 ID（可於應用程式「設定 → 個人資料」中找到）。

Delete my data · 刪除我的資料

In the app: Settings → Profile → Delete my data. It removes your data straight away, except reported BOMs, which are kept for review. No longer have the app? See Delete your BOM account.
在應用程式內：設定 → 個人資料 → 刪除我的資料，即可立即刪除你的資料（被舉報的 BOM 除外，會保留以供處理）。已沒有應用程式？請看刪除 BOM 帳戶。

Report a BOM · 舉報

Open the BOM, tap ⋯ → Report this message and choose a reason. You can also block the sender without learning who they are.
打開該 BOM，按 ⋯ 選擇舉報，然後選擇原因。你亦可以封鎖寄件人，而不會知道對方是誰。
