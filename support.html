Support · 支援

Contact us · 聯絡我們
bomapphelp@gmail.com

We reply within a few days. Please include your anonymous ID (Settings in the app) if your question is about your data.
我們會在數日內回覆。如查詢與你的資料有關，請附上你的匿名 ID（可於應用程式「設定」中找到）。

Delete my data · 刪除我的資料

In the app: Settings → Delete my data. It removes everything straight away.
在應用程式內：設定 → 刪除我的資料，即可立即刪除所有資料。

Report a BOM · 舉報

Open the BOM and tap Report. You can also block the sender without learning who they are.
打開該 BOM 並按「舉報」。你亦可以封鎖寄件人，而不會知道對方是誰。
