Delete your BOM account · 刪除 BOM 帳戶

BOM by Ho Chung Au and Chun Yin Au. You can delete your BOM account and all its data in two ways.

1. In the app (immediate)

Open BOM → Settings → Delete my data. This deletes at once every BOM you sent or received, your BOM friends (on both sides), your QR codes, your usage days and your anonymous account.

2. Without the app (by email)

Email bomapphelp@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my BOM data” and your anonymous ID if you have it (shown in BOM under Settings). We delete everything listed above within 30 days and email you when it is done.

BOM does not collect your name, email or phone number, so without your anonymous ID we cannot tell which data is yours. In that case your data is deleted automatically 12 months after you last used BOM. If you backed up your account with your Apple ID, you can also reinstall BOM, restore the account and delete it in the app.

What is kept

Nothing, except copies in our backups, which are replaced within 5 weeks. A BOM you shared through WhatsApp or another app also stays in that app’s chat, which BOM cannot delete.

中文

BOM 由 Ho Chung Au 及 Chun Yin Au 營運。你可以用以下兩種方法刪除 BOM 帳戶及所有相關資料。

一、在應用程式內（即時）

打開 BOM → 設定 → 刪除我的資料。系統會即時刪除你寄出及收到的所有 BOM、你的 BOM 朋友（雙方的記錄）、你的 QR 碼、使用日子記錄，以及你的匿名帳戶。

二、沒有應用程式（以電郵申請）

請電郵至 bomapphelp@gmail.com，標題寫「Delete my BOM data」，如有匿名 ID 請一併附上（可在 BOM 的「設定」中找到）。我們會在 30 日內刪除以上所有資料，完成後以電郵通知你。

BOM 不會收集你的姓名、電郵或電話號碼，因此沒有匿名 ID 便無法辨認哪些資料屬於你。在這情況下，你的資料會在你最後一次使用 BOM 的 12 個月後自動刪除。如果你曾用 Apple ID 備份帳戶，亦可重新安裝 BOM、還原帳戶，再在應用程式內刪除。

會保留甚麼

除備份副本外，不會保留任何資料；備份副本會在 5 星期內被取代。經 WhatsApp 或其他應用程式分享的 BOM 會留在該應用程式的對話中，BOM 無法刪除。
