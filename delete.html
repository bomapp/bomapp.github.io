Delete your BOM account

BOM by Ho Chung Au. You can delete your BOM account and all its data in two ways.

1. In the app (immediate)

Open BOM → Settings → Profile → Delete my data. This deletes at once every BOM you received and every BOM you sent that nobody has received yet, your BOM friends (on both sides), your QR codes and invite links, your usage days, your private backup of nicknames and blocked senders, your Apple or Google account backup, and your anonymous account.

2. Without the app (by email)

Email bomapphelp@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my BOM data” and your anonymous ID if you have it (shown in BOM under Settings → Profile). We delete everything listed above within 30 days and email you when it is done.

BOM does not collect your name, email or phone number, so without your anonymous ID we cannot tell which data is yours. In that case your data is deleted automatically 12 months after you last used BOM. If you backed up your account with your Apple ID or Google account, you can also reinstall BOM, restore the account and delete it in the app.

What is kept

A reported BOM (one you sent that someone reported, or one you received and reported) is kept for review for up to 12 months, with the link to its sender if it was anonymous, as our privacy policy explains. If an account was banned for misuse, its anonymous ID stays on the list of banned accounts. Nothing else is kept, except copies in our backups, which are replaced within 5 weeks. A BOM you sent that someone already has stays with them, anonymised: your anonymous ID, display name and the tag that linked it to you are removed. A BOM you shared through WhatsApp or another app also stays in that app’s chat, which BOM cannot delete.
