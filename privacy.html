Who we are

BOM collects as little as it can: no phone number, no contacts, no messages you write. The only name it stores is the display name you choose to show, if you set one. This policy covers users in the United Kingdom and in Hong Kong.

BOM is an iPhone app for sending a friend a short, pre-written note of praise — with your name, or anonymously to friends who already use BOM. It is in a test phase in the United Kingdom and Hong Kong.

BOM is run by Ho Chung Au, based in the United Kingdom, and Chun Yin Au, based in Hong Kong. We are joint controllers under UK data protection law and data users under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, and we share responsibility for your data equally. You can contact either or both of us at one address: bomapphelp@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you open BOM it signs you in with a random anonymous ID, not linked to your phone number, email or Apple ID.

On our server: your anonymous ID; your display name, if you set one; for each BOM you share, which pre-written message it is, its code, when it was sent and your display name if set; for each BOM you send to a BOM friend, which message, the friend's anonymous ID and when (an anonymous one carries a random tag, not your ID; the link to you is kept in a private record only you can see); whether each BOM was opened, thanked, skipped or reported, and when; who your BOM friends are and the BOM or QR code that linked you; for each “add BOM friend” QR code you make, your anonymous ID, your display name if set, and when (it works for 24 hours); and how you use the app: first and last day used, the days you used it, your phone's region setting (e.g. HK or GB), BOM's language and your time zone.

Only on your phone: nicknames you type for recipients, senders you have blocked, and which BOMs you have opened.

You become BOM friends with someone when one of you opens a BOM the other sent with their name, or scans the other's QR code. Your BOM friends list is visible only to you.

Only on your phone, never stored or sent: what you type into “Help me pick” (Apple's on-device AI uses it to suggest a ready-made note), and the camera picture when you scan a QR code.

What we never collect

Your phone number, email address or real name (unless you type it as your display name). Your contacts. Any message you write: BOM has only pre-written messages; the one thing you can type is your display name, which is checked against a list of offensive words. Your location (only your phone's region setting, never GPS). Your photos or microphone (the camera is used only while you scan a friend's QR code). Advertising IDs: BOM has no ads, no third-party analytics and no cross-app tracking.

Why we use it

To deliver BOMs, show who sent a named BOM, and show you whether yours were opened or thanked (UK GDPR: contract). To stop misuse through reports, blocking, the name filter and sending limits (legitimate interests). To learn whether BOM works in each market, comparing Hong Kong and the UK using totals only, such as how many BOMs are sent and how many people come back (legitimate interests).

We never build profiles of individuals, and never sell, share or use your data for advertising or direct marketing. In Hong Kong, your data is used only for these purposes, which are directly related to why it was collected.

Where it is stored

Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), run by Google as our data processor, in Google's London region — for users in both the UK and Hong Kong, so Hong Kong users' data is stored outside Hong Kong. Google may process limited technical data outside the UK under its own transfer safeguards. Apart from the two of us, who access it from the UK and Hong Kong, no one else receives BOM data.

How long we keep it

We keep BOM records and BOM friend records for 12 months from when they are created, and your usage record for 12 months from when you last used the app, then delete them within the following month. When the test phase ends, we will delete all test data within 30 days.

Anonymity and safety

BOM is for adults aged 18 and over; if we learn someone under 18 is using it, we delete their data.

BOMs you share yourself are not anonymous: they show your display name if set, and arrive from your own WhatsApp or messaging account. BOMs sent to a BOM friend can be anonymous: they carry no ID of yours, only a random tag, so the recipient can block you without learning who you are. We do not reveal who sent an anonymous BOM unless the law requires it.

Reports are reviewed together with the BOM record. Blocking is stored only on your phone and the sender is not told. Display names are checked against a list of offensive words in English and Chinese; no filter is perfect, so please report any card with an offensive name.

Your rights

In the UK (UK GDPR) you can ask for a copy of your data, or to correct, delete, restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. In Hong Kong (Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance) you can ask for a copy of your personal data (a data access request) and to correct it; we will also delete it on request.

The quickest way to delete everything is Settings → Delete my data, which removes every BOM you sent or received, your wings and your usage days straight away. For other requests, email bomapphelp@gmail.com with your anonymous ID (shown in Settings). We reply within one month.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk) or to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data in Hong Kong (pcpd.org.hk).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and show a notice in the app before the change applies. Questions: bomapphelp@gmail.com.

我們是誰

BOM 盡量少收集資料：不會收集你的電話號碼、通訊錄或你撰寫的訊息。我們唯一儲存的名字，是你自行選擇顯示的名稱（如有設定）。本政策適用於英國及香港用戶。中英文版本如有差異，以英文版本為準。

BOM 是一個 iPhone 應用程式，讓你以預先寫好的短句讚賞朋友——可以署名，亦可以匿名傳送給已使用 BOM 的朋友。BOM 現正於英國及香港進行測試。

BOM 由居於英國的 Ho Chung Au 及居於香港的 Chun Yin Au 共同營運。我們是英國資料保護法例下的共同資料控制者，亦是香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》下的資料使用者，對你的資料承擔同等責任。你可以聯絡我們任何一人或兩人，統一聯絡地址：bomapphelp@gmail.com。

我們收集的資料

打開 BOM 時，系統會以一個隨機的匿名 ID 為你登入。這個 ID 與你的電話號碼、電郵或 Apple ID 沒有任何關聯。

儲存於我們的伺服器：你的匿名 ID；你的顯示名稱（如有設定）；你自行分享的每個 BOM 是哪一句預寫訊息、其代碼、傳送時間及你的顯示名稱（如有）；你傳送給 BOM 朋友的每個 BOM 是哪一句訊息、朋友的匿名 ID 及時間（匿名 BOM 只帶有一個隨機標籤，而不是你的 ID；與你的關聯只存於一份只有你能查看的私人紀錄）；每個 BOM 是否已被開啟、道謝、略過或舉報及其時間；你與哪些人是 BOM 朋友，以及令你們成為朋友的那個 BOM 或 QR code；你製作的每個「加 BOM 朋友」QR code 的匿名 ID、顯示名稱（如有）及時間（有效 24 小時）；以及使用情況：首次及最後使用日期、使用過的日子、手機的地區設定（例如 HK 或 GB）、BOM 的語言及時區。

只存於你的手機：你為收件人輸入的暱稱、你封鎖的寄件人，以及你已開啟的 BOM。

當其中一方開啟另一方署名傳送的 BOM，或掃描另一方的 QR code，你們便成為 BOM 朋友。你的 BOM 朋友名單只有你自己可以查看。

只在你的手機上處理、絕不儲存或傳送：你在「幫我揀」輸入的文字（由 Apple 的裝置端 AI 用來建議預寫句子），以及掃描 QR code 時的相機畫面。

我們不會收集的資料

你的電話號碼、電郵地址或真實姓名（除非你以真名作為顯示名稱）；你的通訊錄；你撰寫的任何訊息（BOM 只有預先寫好的訊息，你唯一可以輸入的是顯示名稱，而系統會以冒犯性字詞名單加以檢查）；你的位置（只收集手機的地區設定，絕不使用 GPS）；你的相片或咪高峰（相機只會在你掃描朋友的 QR code 時使用）；廣告識別碼（BOM 沒有廣告、沒有第三方分析工具，亦不會跨應用程式追蹤你）。

使用目的

送達 BOM、顯示署名 BOM 的寄件人，以及讓你知道你的 BOM 是否已被開啟或道謝（英國法律依據：合約）；透過舉報、封鎖、名稱過濾及傳送上限防止濫用（正當利益）；以總數形式了解 BOM 在各市場的成效並比較香港與英國，例如傳送數量及回來使用的人數（正當利益）。

我們不會為個別用戶建立檔案，亦絕不會出售、分享或把你的資料用於廣告或直接促銷。在香港，你的資料只會用於以上與收集目的直接相關的用途。

儲存地點

BOM 的資料儲存於 Google Firebase（Firestore 及 Firebase Authentication），由 Google 以資料處理者身份營運，位於 Google 的倫敦區域；英國及香港用戶的資料同樣儲存於該處，即香港用戶的資料儲存於香港以外。Google 或會按其自身的跨境保障措施，在英國以外處理有限的技術資料。除我們兩人（分別在英國及香港存取資料）外，沒有其他人會收到 BOM 的資料。

保留期限

BOM 紀錄及 BOM 朋友紀錄自建立起保留 12 個月，使用情況紀錄自你最後一次使用起保留 12 個月，其後會在下一個月內刪除。測試期結束後，我們會在 30 日內刪除所有測試資料。

匿名與安全

BOM 只供 18 歲或以上人士使用；如我們得知有 18 歲以下人士使用，會刪除其資料。

你自行分享的 BOM 不是匿名的：如你設定了顯示名稱，卡上會顯示該名稱，而且該 BOM 是經你自己的 WhatsApp 或通訊帳戶傳送的。傳送給 BOM 朋友的 BOM 可以是匿名的：它不帶有你的 ID，只有一個隨機標籤，讓收件人可以封鎖你而不會知道你是誰。除非法律要求，我們不會披露匿名 BOM 的寄件人。

舉報會連同 BOM 紀錄一併處理。封鎖只儲存於你的手機，寄件人不會收到通知。顯示名稱會以英文及中文的冒犯性字詞名單檢查；過濾無法做到完美，如看到帶有冒犯性名稱的卡，請加以舉報。

你的權利

香港（《個人資料（私隱）條例》）：你可以要求獲取你的個人資料複本（查閱資料要求）及要求改正；我們亦會應你的要求刪除資料。英國（UK GDPR）：你可以要求獲取、改正、刪除、限制或反對使用你的資料，以及以可攜帶的格式獲取資料。

最快的刪除方法是在應用程式內選擇「設定 → 刪除我的資料」，即可立即刪除你傳送或收到的所有 BOM、天使翼及使用紀錄。其他要求請電郵至 bomapphelp@gmail.com，並提供你的匿名 ID（可於「設定」中找到）。我們會在一個月內回覆。

如你不滿意我們處理你資料的方式，可以向香港個人資料私隱專員公署（pcpd.org.hk）或英國資訊專員辦公室（ico.org.uk）投訴。

政策更改及聯絡

如我們修改本政策，會更新頁首的日期，並在更改生效前於應用程式內通知你。查詢：bomapphelp@gmail.com。
